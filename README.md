# Multiphase Flow Analysis

![Cover](cover.png)

Engineering studies focused on multiphase flow behavior,
particle interaction and industrial filtration systems.

The objective is not only to visualize particle motion,
but to understand interaction between phases,
flow stability and industrial process efficiency.

---

# Engineering focus

• particle behavior  
• industrial filtration  
• dust separation  
• liquid-gas interaction  
• sedimentation systems  
• multiphase flow dynamics  
• filtration optimization  

---

# Engineering approach

These studies focus on practical engineering understanding,
process interpretation and optimization opportunities.

but to better understand industrial process dynamics
inside engineering systems.
